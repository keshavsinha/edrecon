# Notice on Intended Use

EDRecon is an educational tool. It is intended for use against systems you
own or for which you hold explicit written authorisation to test.

Unauthorised scanning of computer systems is a criminal offence in most
jurisdictions. In India, the Information Technology Act, 2000 (principally
sections 43 and 66) applies.

The MIT licence grants rights over the software. It does not grant permission
to test any system, and it is not a defence against misuse.
